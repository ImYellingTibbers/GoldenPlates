# Golden Plates

A searchable reference of every commandment and promise given by Presidents of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, drawn from their sermons and teachings while in office.

Currently covers Brigham Young and John Taylor (2 of 17 presidents); more will be added as each prophet's review completes.

Live site: served via GitHub Pages from `docs/`.

Sources: Journal of Discourses (public domain) and the *Teachings of Presidents of the Church* manuals (excerpted, Church-copyrighted).
